About

Engineer, with an economist's habit

I study computer science with a minor in economics, and the economics shows up in how I build. Before I write code, I ask what a mistake would cost and who pays for it. That question decided SafeShare's no-server rule and MythTrace's attack tests.

I'm most interested in AI for high-stakes industries and in how people actually use these systems. I've also worked on the business side, in sales at Metvy, and I like being the person who can talk to users and then go build the thing.
